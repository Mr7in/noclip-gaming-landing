FAQ

Questions operators ask.

What is a skin?

A skin is a sport on top of the shared engine: its match rules and simulation, its fictional league and players, its markets, and its art and sound. The engine underneath (odds, settlement, wallet, collection, season pass, operator switches) is identical in every title.

Which sports are live?

Basketball (Backcourt) and ice hockey (Backcheck) are playable today. Football and volleyball are coming next, and further sports are shaped by operator demand.

Can the game carry our brand?

Yes. The engine can be re-skinned with your colours, logo, typography, arena branding, card art and league naming, up to a fully custom league. Gameplay, maths and certification stay the same across skins.

How long is a match?

Two to five minutes of play, with speed controls from 0.25x to 4x.

Do players have to bet to play?

No. Every match is playable and rewarding without a stake. Betting is an optional layer on top.

What markets are offered?

Each sport has markets that fit how fans bet it: winner, handicap, totals, player performance and a bet builder, before the match and at every break.

Can buying cards buy better betting returns?

No. Card strength changes the odds a player is offered, not the house edge. Features that could indirectly affect returns can be switched off per market.

What stake range is supported?

€0.10 to €500 per bet by default, configurable by the operator.

Is it a real league?

No. Every skin runs a fictional league, so there are no data feed costs, no licensing of real athletes and no integrity risk from real-world events.

Where should it live in our lobby?

We recommend a dedicated page in your main navigation, plus a tile in the casino and sports lobbies. These are games players return to daily, so a permanent home works better than a rotating slot position.

Can we start small?

Yes. Launch in one market with the features you choose, measure retention, and widen from there.
