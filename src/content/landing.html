Maths and fairness

A stronger team changes the odds, never the return.

Better cards, tactics and boosters make a player's team more likely to win, and the price shortens to match. Expected return per bet stays at the configured RTP for every market, whoever plays whom. That is what lets a skill-flavoured meta sit on top of a certifiable game.

One match, every bet

All bets settle from the single simulated match the player watches. No hidden second draw.

Priced by the same engine

Odds come from a Monte Carlo of the match engine plus a fixed margin per market.

Changes before prices

Tactics and boosters lock before odds open, at tip-off or a break. Nothing changes mid-play.

Built for the lab

CSPRNG throughout, RTP verified over millions of simulated matches, math report for an accredited test lab.

Before you list it

Questions operators ask.

Are Coin purchases allowed in my market?

That depends on your licence. Coins are not redeemable, and real money never buys a random item directly. If purchases aren't allowed, switch them off: the game runs on earned Coins with repriced packs.

How are Coin sales treated for tax?

Coin sales are a separate line from wagering. Agree the booking route and tax treatment with your finance team before switching them on.

Does skill or squad strength break the house edge?

No. A stronger team gets shorter odds in exact proportion, so the return per bet stays at the configured RTP for every matchup.

Are rewards an inducement to gamble?

Rewards come from playing matches, with or without a bet. Rewards tied to wagering are off by default, and no leaderboard ranks money.

Is it certified?

Not yet. The math engine and RTP verification are done, and RNG and game-maths certification with an accredited lab is next. Launch partners get the report first.

What do I need to integrate?

Nothing new: Backcourt Manager uses the standard launch and wallet flow and your existing player balance.
