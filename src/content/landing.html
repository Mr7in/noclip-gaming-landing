How a match works

One match, four decisions, up to four betting windows.

  1. Pre-game.Set the lineup, pick up to two boosters, choose tactics. Odds are priced from thousands of simulations of this exact matchup.
  2. Q1 break.Read the box score, adjust the plan, bet again at live prices or cash out.
  3. Q2 break.Same again. The half-time decision is the biggest one.
  4. Q3 break.Last chance to adjust and bet before the final quarter.

The systems behind it

Card collection with four tiers.

Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary, with real scarcity (only 50 copies of each Legendary). Duplicates turn into Shards, so no pack is wasted.

Club albums.

A sticker book for each of the 50 clubs. Completing one permanently boosts that club's players in your lineup. Collector milestones run all the way to 500 placed stickers.

Divisions.

Ten divisions, 20 teams each, weekly reset. Top three go up, bottom three go down. A progress bar always shows how many wins to the promotion line.

Fixture list with rivalries.

Three open matches at a time (easier, even, harder). A club that beats you comes back as a Revenge match. Beat all 19 rivals for a clean sweep reward.

Daily streak with a shield.

A 7-day reward ladder that grows week on week, plus a streak shield so one missed day doesn't wipe out a habit.

Levels and chests.

Every level-up opens a chest that can upgrade as it opens. Milestone levels unlock kits, arenas and badges.

Season pass.

Measured in matches, not calendar weeks, so casual and heavy players both finish it.

Team identity.

Custom kits, arenas and badges that opponents see. Cosmetics only, no effect on stats.

FAQ

Questions operators ask.

How long is a match?

Two to five minutes of play, with speed controls from 0.25x to 4x.

Do players have to bet to play?

No. Every match is playable and rewarding without a stake. Betting is an optional layer on top.

What markets are offered?

Match winner, handicap, total points, captain's points and a bet builder, before tip-off and at every quarter break.

Can buying cards buy better betting returns?

No. Card strength changes the odds a player is offered, not the house edge. Features that could indirectly affect returns can be switched off per market.

What stake range is supported?

€0.10 to €500 per bet by default, configurable by the operator.

Is it a real league?

No. Backcourt League is a fictional league of 50 clubs and 500 players, so there are no data feed costs, no licensing of real athletes and no integrity risk from real-world events.
